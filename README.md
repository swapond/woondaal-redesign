# Woondaal King Kebab — Website Redesign

> **🌐 Live Website**: [**https://swapond.github.io/woondaal-redesign/**](https://swapond.github.io/woondaal-redesign/)

A minimal, editorial restaurant website redesign for **Woondaal King Kebab**, located in East Zindabazar, Sylhet, Bangladesh.

Designed with a calm, white-first aesthetic, refined typography, authentic Google Maps reviews, Foodpanda online ordering integration, and an interactive cart with direct WhatsApp checkout.

## 🍽️ Key Features

- **Editorial Hero Banner**: Atmospheric culinary visuals with quick actions to *View Menu* and *Get Directions*.
- **Interactive Menu Catalog**: Categorized dishes (Kebabs, Chicken, Beef, Mutton, Vegetable, Rice, Naan, Soup, Salad), real-time search, accurate Sylhet prices in ৳ [BDT], and 1-tap cart additions.
- **Cart Drawer & WhatsApp Ordering**: Slide-over drawer with item counter, quantity controls, dining preferences (Dine-in, Takeaway, Delivery), and instant redirect to WhatsApp (+880 1717-020505) with preformatted itemized orders.
- **Foodpanda Online Delivery**: Dedicated home-delivery callout card and direct ordering links with 4.5 ★ rating.
- **Guest Experiences & Reviews**: Testimonials from Sylhet locals, Google Local Guides, and overseas travelers with 5-star ratings.
- **Visit & Contact**: Address (Barshon-88, Main Road, East Zindabazar), opening hours (12:00 PM – 11:30 PM), direct WhatsApp chat, and interactive Google Maps embed.
- **Responsive & Accessible**: Touch-first mobile navigation drawer, accessible modals, and Schema.org Restaurant JSON-LD metadata for SEO.
